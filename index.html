About Me

Hello! I'm Jacob, a passionate 27 year old Game Designer based in Stockholm. I started my Game Dev journey back in 2021 and I've been completely hooked ever since. My main focus is anything Technical or Systems/Gameplay related. I love quick prototyping and iterating on ideas until they feel just right.

I got my bachelor's degree in Game Design & Scripting in 2024 and I am currently studying Game Design at Futuregames in Stockholm. My internship period is just around the corner, so I'm on the lookout for any game design related opportunities!

Some of my favorite games, that are also huge sources of inspiration for me, are League of Legends, World of Warcraft, Baldur's Gate 3, V Rising, and the Legend of Zelda series.
