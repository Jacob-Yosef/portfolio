Viscerate

17 People 7 Weeks Unreal Engine 2025

Overview

Viscerate is a fast-paced FPS roguelite focused on fluid movement, modular builds, and punishing-but-fair combat. Players craft powerful synergies through weapon evolutions and over 40 upgrade modules while fighting through a gritty, bio-punk industrial world.

This game was developed during a 7-week group project and my main responsibilities were technical & gameplay design, in addition to some level design.

Gameplay

Gameplay Design Philosophy

My gameplay focus was on enhancing second-to-second combat by giving players tools that reward aggression and improvisation. I aimed to increase player expression through environmental interaction and movement systems.

Explosive Barrels

The Feature

I introduced explosive barrels as environmental hazards to make combat spaces more interactive, and for some gameplay variety.

The Problem

Playtests showed that completely stationary barrels were a bit unutilized. Often, players had to lure enemies into the barrels range, which disrupted the fast paced nature of the game.

The Solution

I allowed players to melee-kick barrels, launching them into enemies. Making them explode on impact turned them into a fast, skill-based combat tool that rewarded aggressive repositioning and felt very satisfying.

Jump Pads

The Feature

To complement the game's vertical level design, I implemented jump pads to improve traversal and help players maintain combat momentum.

The Problem

During testing, it became clear that players could abuse the jump pads to endlessly kite enemies from above, removing any sense of danger.

The Solution

I added a short cooldown to each jump pad after use, which is communicated through a simple blinking material effect. Players could now use them to get out of immediate danger, while not being able to completely abuse it.

Boss & Mini-Boss Design

Initially, I was responsible only for the main boss and elite enemy design. Late in development, I also took over full behavior and ability implementation due to production challenges and time limitations.

Brute Mini-Boss

The Brute was originally planned as a stronger standard enemy, but its signature charge-based ability conflicted with the game's level design which started becoming more vertical. I therefore restructured it into a mini-boss with a dedicated arena room.

Charge

The Brute charges at the player, dealing heavy damage on impact. A well-timed dodge causes it to crash into a wall which stuns it, creating an opening for attack by exposing a weak point.

Final Boss - The Collective Heart

Due to time and resource constraints, we opted out of an overly complex final boss design. However, I thought it was important to have an appropriately climatic ending to the game. The Collective Heart was the solution - a passive boss that serves as the source that sustains all the enemies in the game.

The fight is structured into 3 phases, combining enemy waves, environmental attacks, and escalating beam attacks to create a simple, yet high-intensity finale.

Raining projectiles

The boss rains projectiles that the player has to dodge, forcing the player to stay mobile.
