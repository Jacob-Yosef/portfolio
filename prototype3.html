Boss Fight Prototype

A technical showcase of boss encounters with varied combat mechanics

Solo 3 Weeks Unity 2024

Overview

This prototype explores boss encounters with distinct themes and abilities. The goal was to design and implement different boss fights, each featuring unique mechanics and playstyles, while incorporating status effects, stacking mechanics, and debuffs.

Melee Attack

Slashes the sword and deals damage to hit enemies. Charging the melee attacks will swing the sword and deal an increased amount of AoE damage.

Player Abilities

Players can customize their hotbar with a set of available skills, via a spellbook. The player has melee attacks, projectiles attacks and a dash.
