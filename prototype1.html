Alpine Adventures

7 People 8 Weeks Unity 2023

Overview

A narrative exploration game set in the Swiss Alps, where players travel with a dog companion while retracing a journey once taken by their late father. The goal is to visit the same locations nad make the hike up to the mountain top. The experience focuses mainly on atmosphere and storytelling, but has traversal, puzzle and survival challenges as well.
