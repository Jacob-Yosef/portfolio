Hoverboarder

Solo 3 Weeks Unreal Engine 2026

Overview

Hoverboarder is an open-world hoverboarding prototype developed during a 3-week Systems Design course. Rather than polishing a single mechanic or visuals to perfection, the goal was to design a cohesive set of systems that support a complete gameplay loop in an open-world context.

The scope of the open world is intentionally small, but the focus is on systemic depth and flexibility. All major systems are built around a single core pillar: physics-based hoverboarding traversal that enables emergent gameplay and player expression.

Design Goals

  • Design a physics-based traversal system that feels smooth and satisfying
  • Build a progression system that reinforces mastery of movement and enhances the hoverboarding experience
  • Create a complete open-world gameplay loop with limited development time
  • Support player choice, creativity, and expression
  • Create modular / data-driven systems to enable quick iteration and scalability

Physics-Based Hoverboard Movement

The main system and the heart of the prototype is the Hoverboard Movement. The hoverboard uses multiple line traces to detect distance from the ground. Each trace calculates a push force based on that distance, allowing the board to dynamically adjust to the terrain.

Progression & Upgrade Systems

Stat-Based Upgrades

The hoverboard features two core upgradable stats: Acceleration and Rotation Speed. Upgrading them is essential for long distance jumps and making tight corners.

Active Ability Upgrades

Hoverboard abilities were designed to create distinct movement options that target different traversal challenges. Each ability opens up new ways to navigate the world and tackle races.

Nitro Boost

Provides additional forward velocity with limited charge. Useful in many areas of the game, especially speed-focused races and long jumps.

Open World Exploration & Resources

Scrap Collection

Scrap serves as the primary upgrade currency and is scattered throughout the world. Some scrap is placed openly, while other pickups require timed jumps or precise movement. This encourages players to explore between races, enhancing the second-to-second gameplay.

Environmental Interaction: Turtles

Turtles are scattered throughout the world and act as dynamic environmental elements. When collided with, they launch the player into the air. Depending on the situation, turtles can function as obstacles to avoid or tools for reaching new locations, which is explored in certain races.

NPC & Dialogue System

Flexible Dialogue Framework

To support the open world, mainly the races, I implemented a dialogue system designed for rapid prototyping. Dialogue content is driven through data tables, and any NPC can be assigned dialogue with minimal setup.

Dialogue States & Events

The system uses a dialogue state enum to track NPC states and an event system triggered by player responses. This allows dialogue choices to affect NPC behavior and unlock different outcomes, while remaining flexible enough to support future expansion.

This data-driven approach allowed me to quickly populate the world with NPCs, provide context for progression and direct players toward races and upgrade stations.

Race System Design

To complete a race, players must pass through all checkpoints, reach the finish, and beat the specified time. Successful completion rewards Scrap, blueprints, and/or cosmetic unlocks.

Each race was designed to highlight a different aspect of hoverboarding mastery. The modular race component system allowed me to quickly create varied experiences for the different races.

Player Expression: Hoverboard Customization

To complement overall progression, I added a hoverboard customization system. Players can change the board's material, hover VFX, and nitro VFX. I wanted to reinforce player expression, which felt especially important in an open-world setting.

Customization options are unlocked through hoverboard level progression and race completion, allowing players to create a hoverboard that feels unique and reflects their achievements.

World Map & Player Guidance

Even with a relatively small open world, finding races and upgrade stations could be difficult without guidance. To address this, I created a map system that displays all races in the world and uses color-coding to differentiate available and locked races.

Map Features:

  • Dynamic Race Markers: Available races highlighted in one color, locked races in another
  • Requirement Tooltips: Hovering over locked races displays required upgrades/stats
  • Upgrade Stations: Shop locations clearly indicated for easy access


The map connects all the systems and helps with pacing by guiding the player to points of interest.
